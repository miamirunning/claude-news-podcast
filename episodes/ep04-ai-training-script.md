## Workflow two: How I AI
AVA: Workflow two. On How I AI's October nineteenth episode, creator Brooke Lacey showed Claire Vo how Claude Code edits her TikToks in about fifteen seconds.
ANDREW: She films one long take on her phone and AirDrops it to her Mac. Claude Code runs FFmpeg, Whisper, and Python locally, using silence detection and word timings for jump cuts, face tracking for framing, and one-word-at-a-time captions.
AVA: The clever bit: the edit is a text file of segments, each with a start, an end, and a shot size. Edit the text, and you get new cuts from the same footage.
ANDREW: The caveat: it works because Brooke spent years learning what good timing looks like, then wrote that taste down for the AI.
AVA: And in June, Claire built an avatar of herself in Google Flow and made a one-minute promo in about fifteen minutes. Her verdict: shareable, not polished. Calm shots worked, expressive ones got uncanny.
